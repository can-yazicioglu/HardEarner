What is stored

Your holdings, connection keys, purchase history, analysis, alerts and performance records are encrypted in the browser before cloud storage. Two previous encrypted cloud revisions are retained for recovery. Your browser also keeps encrypted pending saves and recovery copies. Public market quotes may be cached on this device without encryption; these contain no account keys or balances.

Connected accounts and data providers

When you connect or sync an account, its read-only credentials are decrypted in the browser and sent over HTTPS to HardEarner’s Cloudflare service. The service temporarily uses them to read the selected provider and does not intentionally log or persist plaintext credentials. This is encryption at rest; the relay does see credentials during requests. Twelve Data keys follow the same process. Public chart streams connect directly to the selected exchange. Provider region and IP restrictions still apply.

Research widgets

Research embeds public TradingView charts, screeners and news in separate cross-origin frames. TradingView receives your IP address, browser information and the public symbols you view, and may use its own cookies or storage. Your holdings, trade logs and API keys are not passed to these widgets. Public overview data comes from CoinGecko, CoinLore, CoinPaprika and DefiLlama. See TradingView’s privacy policy. Prices and news remain subject to provider availability and delays.

Optional research data

Bybit supplies funding rates, open interest and observed liquidation events. Liquidation WebSockets connect directly from your browser. Public Hyperliquid addresses you choose to watch are sent through the HardEarner service to Hyperliquid. Optional CoinGlass, Glassnode, FRED, CoinGecko Demo, DefiLlama Pro and Financial Modeling Prep keys are encrypted in your vault, temporarily decrypted for requests, and sent through the HTTPS relay only to the selected provider. Provider credentials and licensed results are not placed in a shared public cache. Research preferences, metric alerts, ETF revision observations and limited public-position changes are encrypted in the vault. Providers receive the selected assets, time ranges, public addresses or filters and normal request metadata; they do not receive your portfolio or exchange credentials. Their plan, IP, region and licensing restrictions apply.

Research Lab uses public Deribit options, Alternative.me sentiment, SEC filings and DefiLlama data. Research journals, layouts and public watchlists are encrypted in the same vault; they are not automatically sent to an LLM or developer. Theme preference alone is stored unencrypted. Opening Research from an unlocked parent transfers a non-extractable encryption key in memory to the exact same-origin child after a one-use handshake. It does not send your password or save a persistent login. Explicit lock and password rotation lock the account’s other tabs; refresh and direct visits still require unlock.

Your controls

Lock the vault from the header. Change its password in Settings; old exported backups retain their original password. A full encrypted backup includes connection keys and history. The separate manual-portfolio JSON export and CSV are unencrypted. Keep exports private. Disconnecting removes live credentials from the current vault; prior encrypted backups may retain them, and performance evidence without usable keys remains to explain past results. Revoke unused keys on the provider too.

Devices and notifications

Browsers signed in to the same private account share the same encrypted vault. The installed Windows app currently has separate data. Web alerts require a visible, online, unlocked page and stop when the browser closes or suspends it. System notifications depend on browser permission. No orders, withdrawals or payments are placed.

Support and deletion requests

Closing the browser or clearing its storage does not delete the cloud vault. Contact can.yazicioglu.p@gmail.com for support, privacy or deletion requests. Do not send passwords, API secrets or backup files. In-app issue reports download to your device and are not sent automatically.
