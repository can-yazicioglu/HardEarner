HardEarner for Windows

Your money. Your device.

Privacy notice · Windows desktop version 2.14 · 3 October 2026

HardEarner is a personal savings tracker made by Can Yazicioglu. This notice covers the Windows desktop app. The web/mobile app has its own privacy notice.

What stays on your computer

Manual holdings, purchase costs, connected balances and supported transaction history, account credentials, daily performance observations and minimal transaction evidence, investment listings, optional market-data credentials, drawings, notes, alerts and settings are stored on your Windows device. HardEarner does not upload this portfolio database to a HardEarner account or server. There is no automatic desktop-to-phone sync.

The portfolio and connection records are encrypted using Windows-user-protected keys. The app password controls access to the interface; its verification value is stored locally. It does not encrypt exported backups or protect against someone who controls your Windows account. There is no email password reset. Public market-price caches and ordinary application diagnostic files may remain unencrypted.

Optional connected accounts

If you connect an exchange or supported bank, the app sends authenticated read requests directly to that provider to obtain balances and supported transaction history. API keys, access tokens or request signatures needed for authentication are processed by the provider. Secret signing keys are used locally where the provider's protocol allows; credential formats and permissions vary by provider. Use read-only credentials without trading or withdrawal permissions. HardEarner has no trading, payment, transfer or withdrawal controls.

Supported connections include Binance.com, Binance TR, Kraken, OKX, Coinbase, BtcTurk, Paribu, Trading 212 Invest/Stocks ISA, and personal developer access to Monzo and Starling. Availability and wallet/history coverage vary. Optional approved UK banking setups use TrueLayer and the bank's consent page. A bank password is never requested by HardEarner. Account synchronization continues while the app is open or running in the tray. It stops when the app or computer is off.

Optional public-wallet addresses and statement imports

If you add a public wallet address, HardEarner sends that address to mempool.space for Bitcoin or the relevant Blockscout network service for Ethereum, Base, Arbitrum One, OP Mainnet or Polygon PoS. These providers receive the address, network and normal request metadata. Address balances and supported transaction history are saved locally. Never enter private keys or recovery phrases. Wallet coverage is limited to supported address-level balances and transfers; it is not universal wallet, NFT, staking or DeFi coverage.

Conversion statements you select or paste are parsed on this device. Imported transaction records are saved in the encrypted connection store. The app may request public historical asset prices and fiat exchange rates using the asset symbols and dates/times to estimate entry values and translate capital into USD. It does not upload the statement file to those services.

Internet requests and third parties

Market prices, chart history and live feeds are requested from services such as Binance, OKX, Kraken, CoinGecko, Coinbase Exchange, Frankfurter, Gold API and Twelve Data. These providers receive the requested markets and normal network information such as your IP address. Account providers receive account-specific authenticated requests only when you choose to connect. Their privacy policies govern their own processing.

If you configure a personal Twelve Data key, it is encrypted on your device and sent directly to Twelve Data with your selected listing or search. Coverage, delay and request limits depend on your provider plan. You can clear the key in Stocks & funds → Data settings.

This information website is hosted by Cloudflare, which processes normal request metadata to deliver and protect it. Microsoft processes Store acquisition, distribution, update and diagnostic information under its own privacy statement. HardEarner does not include advertising SDKs or send an app analytics feed to the developer.

Research widgets

The Research window embeds public TradingView charts, screeners, calendars and news in separate cross-origin frames. TradingView receives your IP address, browser information and the public symbols you view, and may use its own cookies or storage. Your holdings, execution details and API keys are not passed to these widgets. Public overview metrics are requested from CoinGecko, CoinPaprika and DefiLlama. See TradingView privacy. Widget availability, refresh and market-data delays depend on the provider.

Notifications and support

Windows notifications can show price-alert and balance-change details. While the app is password-locked, these notifications use generic text. Other people who can see your unlocked desktop may see notifications. Notification settings are available in the app and Windows.

A support report is saved locally and includes the app version, Windows version, market-feed availability and the description you enter. It is not sent automatically. If you email support, the developer and email providers process your email address, message and any attachments to answer your request. Never send API secrets, passwords or unredacted financial backups. Support correspondence is kept only as needed to address the issue and reasonable follow-up; you may request its deletion.

Backups, retention and deletion

JSON and CSV exports are unencrypted. Keep them private and make your own backups. Local records remain until you remove them. Disconnecting an account removes it from the active connection store, but the encrypted performance book retains minimal account labels and transaction evidence (without API credentials) so historical daily results and fees remain explainable. Encrypted previous-version and upgrade backups may also contain earlier records. Revoke the API key at the provider to stop its future use. A full local deletion includes the active data folder and any backups or exports you created. Settings → Open data folder shows the active folder. Quit the app before managing those files. Store uninstall may remove package-local files; legacy desktop data and exported backups can remain. The developer cannot recover or remotely erase a portfolio that was never uploaded.
